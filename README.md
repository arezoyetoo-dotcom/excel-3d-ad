## ⚡ Superpowers & Features

1. **Interactive 3D WebGL Spreadsheet Monolith (Three.js):**
   - **4 Architectural Layers:** Raw Ingestion Base, Data Normalization, Formula Pipeline, and Executive 3D KPI Dashboard.
   - **Chaos vs. Pristine Morphing:** Smoothly transforms between broken, tilted, corrupt `#REF!` / `#DIV/0!` state and an immaculate, aligned emerald/cyan crystal architecture.
   - **Explode Layers Mode:** Expands the 4 layers vertically in 3D space with annotated telemetry.
   - **3D Financial KPI Pillars:** Extrudes interactive 3D bar chart columns (Net Revenue, Gross Margin, Burn Multiple, EBITDA Forecast).
   - **Formula Conduits:** Flowing neon Bezier energy conduits symbolizing dynamic `=XLOOKUP` & `LAMBDA` pipelines.
   - **Interactive Raycasting & Cell Inspector:** Hovering over individual 3D cells reveals live structural diagnostics.

2. **Interactive "Before vs. After" Forensic Split Slider:**
   - Real-time draggable comparison between unformatted, bloated, error-riddled sheets and executive engineered models.

3. **Spreadsheet Waste & ROI Calculator:**
   - Dynamic sliders for Team Size, Weekly Hours Wasted, and Fully-Loaded Hourly Rate.
   - Computes live annual capital bleed, reclaimed hours, and exact payback period in business days.

4. **60-Second Interactive Diagnostic & Quote Wizard:**
   - 3-step automated triage assessing primary bottleneck, sheet scale, and turnaround urgency.
   - Delivers an instant structural health report, recommended package, and transparent price estimate.

5. **Transparent Fixed-Price Service Fleet:**
   - **Tier 1: Rapid Triage & Cleanup ($1,650)** — 48-hour emergency sprint for broken, bloated sheets.
   - **Tier 2: Executive Financial & KPI Model ($3,450)** — 3-statement forecast, scenario switchers, dynamic dashboards.
   - **Tier 3: Full-Stack Automation & ETL Pipeline ($5,800)** — PowerQuery, Python in Excel, automated CSV/API ingestion.

6. **Procedural Web Audio Synthesizer:**
   - Zero external audio files. Synthesized crystal chimes, low-frequency glitch buzzes, spatial explosion sweeps, and tactile click sounds.

7. **Zero-Friction Booking & NDA Vault:**
   - Confidential booking modal with instant confirmation simulation and mutual NDA protection.
