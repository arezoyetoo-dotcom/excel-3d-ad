Privacy Policy & Data Governance Protocol

Last Revised: September 2026 • Version 2.4 (Enterprise Hardened)

1. Our Core Commitment: Zero Data Ingestion

At SheetFix 3D, we operate under strict institutional confidentiality guidelines. We understand that your financial models, executive payroll records, and operational pipelines contain mission-critical proprietary intelligence.

We strictly guarantee: Your spreadsheets, formulas, macro code, and datasets are never fed into public machine learning models or LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot). All architectural reviews are conducted in air-gapped cleanroom environments by human Senior Excel Architects.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum required information to deliver bespoke engineering services:

  • Identity & Contact: Full name, verified corporate email address, and optional Telegram handle (@Shy1ohmy).
  • Project Specifications: Architecture tier selected, spreadsheet volume, and technical descriptions of formula errors or automation objectives.
  • Integrity Proofs: Client-side SHA-256 checksums generated upon delivery to ensure zero unauthorized alteration.

3. 7-Day Ephemeral Storage Policy

Any workbooks, CSV files, or interim calculation models provided during your engagement are stored on encrypted ephemeral volumes. Exactly 7 calendar days following client verification and sign-off, all workbook copies are permanently purged via multi-pass cryptographic wipe routines.

4. Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

Prior to opening or inspecting any workbook file, SheetFix 3D executes an upfront legally binding mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement protecting both parties against unauthorized disclosure, reverse-engineering, or data duplication.

5. Cookies and Telemetry

We do not use invasive third-party tracking or advertising pixel networks. We use essential local storage cookies exclusively to persist your authenticated session and language preferences.

6. Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Regardless of your jurisdiction, you retain unconditional rights to inspect, export, or immediately purge all personal records and intake project histories associated with your account.
